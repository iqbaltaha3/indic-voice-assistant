# Telangana & Andhra Pradesh Land Records — Dharani, Pahani

## Pahani (also called Adangal in this region)
The village-level land record showing survey number, extent, ownership,
and cultivation details for agricultural land -- the regional equivalent
of Karnataka's RTC or Maharashtra's 7/12 extract.

## Dharani
Telangana's unified, fully online land records and registration portal
(launched to replace the earlier separate registration and revenue record
systems). It is intended to combine land registration and mutation into a
single online process. Because it is a newer, actively evolving system,
buyers should independently verify current record status rather than
relying only on cached/printed copies, since the platform's rules and
interfaces have changed over time.

## Due-diligence tip specific to this region
Since Telangana's system ties registration and mutation together more
tightly than most states, a completed Dharani transaction is a stronger
signal than in states where mutation can lag registration by months or
years -- but it is still important to independently confirm there is no
pending litigation or family dispute not reflected in the portal, since
land records generally do not capture unregistered/informal claims.
