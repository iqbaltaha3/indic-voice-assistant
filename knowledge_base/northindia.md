# North India Land Records — Khasra, Khatauni, Bhulekh (UP, Bihar, MP, and similar states)

## Khasra
A field-level record showing details of an individual plot of agricultural
land -- its number, area, soil classification, and the crop grown, similar
in purpose to Karnataka's RTC or Maharashtra's 7/12 extract. It is
maintained plot-by-plot.

## Khatauni
A landholder-level record (rather than plot-level) that lists all the
plots (khasra numbers) held by a particular person or family within a
village, along with their combined area and revenue liability. Khasra and
Khatauni together form the core of the land record system across much of
the Hindi-belt states.

## Bhulekh
The general term (and the name of several states' online portals, e.g. UP
Bhulekh, Bihar Bhumi) for digitized land records that let people check
Khasra/Khatauni details online. Availability and depth of digitization
varies significantly by state and even by district.

## Jamabandi (used in this region as well as Punjab/Haryana)
A periodically updated master record of rights showing the registered
owner/tenant of each plot, similar in function to Khatauni, historically
prepared once every few years by the village patwari (revenue official).

## Due-diligence tip specific to this region
Land records in parts of North India have historically had more manual
record-keeping and slower digitization than states like Karnataka or
Maharashtra, so it is especially important to obtain a physically certified
copy from the local Tehsil/revenue office (not just a printout) and to
verify directly with the patwari or Lekhpal (village-level revenue
official) that no pending mutation or dispute exists, rather than relying
solely on an online portal.
