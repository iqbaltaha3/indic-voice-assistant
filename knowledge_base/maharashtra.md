# Maharashtra Land Records — 7/12 Extract & Property Card

## 7/12 Extract (Saat-Baara Utara)
The primary land record document for agricultural and some rural land in
Maharashtra. Named after two registers ("Form 7" and "Form 12") that were
historically combined into one extract. It shows the survey/gat number,
area, the registered owner's name(s), the type of tenure, and details of
crops cultivated. It also lists any outstanding loans, mortgages, or other
encumbrances registered against the land in the "other rights" column --
this column is one of the most important things to check before buying.

## Property Card (for urban land)
For land within municipal/urban limits in Maharashtra, ownership is
recorded through a Property Card (maintained by the City Survey
department) rather than a 7/12 extract. It shows the City Survey (CTS)
number, area, and registered holder details.

## Mutation Entry (Ferfar)
Called "Ferfar" in Maharashtra -- the mutation entry recorded on the 7/12
extract whenever ownership changes (sale, inheritance, gift). Buyers
should confirm that the mutation entry reflecting their purchase has been
made and that no pending/disputed mutation entries exist on the record,
since a pending entry can indicate an unresolved ownership dispute.

## Due-diligence tip specific to Maharashtra
Always check the "other rights" (itar hakk) column on the 7/12 extract --
this is where mortgages, tenancy rights, and litigation-related notices
are recorded, and a clean main entry can still hide an encumbrance listed
only in this column.
