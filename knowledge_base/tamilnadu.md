# Tamil Nadu Land Records — Patta, Chitta, Adangal

## Patta
The primary ownership document in Tamil Nadu, issued by the revenue
department, showing the registered owner of a specific survey number of
land. A patta is strong evidence of ownership but, like a khata elsewhere,
is a revenue record rather than itself a title-transferring document -- the
underlying sale/gift/partition deed is what legally transfers title, and
the patta should be updated (via a patta transfer application) to reflect
the new owner after a purchase.

## Chitta
A landholding record that consolidates all the survey numbers/extent of
land held by a particular person within a village, similar in role to
Khatauni in North India.

## Adangal (also called A-Register extract)
A detailed field-level register showing survey number, area, classification
of land (wet/dry/garden), and cultivation details -- roughly analogous to
a Khasra record.

## Due-diligence tip specific to Tamil Nadu
Confirm the patta has actually been transferred into the buyer's name
after purchase (this is a separate application process from registering
the sale deed) -- a very common gap is buyers registering the sale deed but
never completing the patta transfer, which can cause complications with
future resale, loans, or property tax payment.
