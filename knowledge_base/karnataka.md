# Karnataka Land Records — RTC / Bhoomi

## RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops)
Karnataka's core land record document for agricultural land, also known as
Pahani. It shows the survey number, extent (area) of land, the registered
owner/cultivator's name, the type of soil and crop grown, and any loans or
charges registered against the land. This is issued and maintained through
the state's "Bhoomi" computerized land records system.

## Bhoomi
The name of Karnataka's online land records portal and digitized RTC
system. It allows checking RTC extracts, mutation status, and survey
records online for agricultural land, reducing (though not eliminating)
the scope for manual record tampering that was common before
digitization.

## Khata (Karnataka-specific: A Khata vs B Khata)
For urban/municipal property in Karnataka (issued by BBMP or other local
bodies rather than Bhoomi), an "A Khata" indicates the property is in a
fully approved, compliant layout and is eligible for building permits and
bank loans. A "B Khata" indicates an irregular or unauthorized layout --
such properties can often still be bought/sold but typically cannot get
formal bank financing or full building-plan approval until regularized,
and they carry meaningfully higher legal risk.

## Due-diligence tip specific to Karnataka
For agricultural land, always cross-check the RTC extract's survey number
and owner name against the sale deed and encumbrance certificate. For
converted (non-agricultural) urban land, confirm a "land conversion order"
(from agricultural to non-agricultural use) exists where required --
buying converted-but-unapproved land is a common source of disputes.
